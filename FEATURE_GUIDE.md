# Quick guide to the new controls

### Camera
Press TAKE PHOTO or RECORD VIDEO and allow access. Select COLOR, NIGHT, or HEAT along the top of the capture dialog. Capture/record, preview, then choose SEND TO CHAT or PLACE ON MAP. The camera is off when the dialog is closed.

### Map image effects
Choose COLOR, NIGHT VISION or HEAT above the map. These change the displayed fictional reference map only.

### Small versus big reports
Select SMALL REPORT, tap the map, enter a reason, and save. At 100% these marks are hidden to reduce clutter; zoom to about 170% to reveal them. For an always-visible mark, select BIG INCIDENT or choose LARGE INCIDENT in a normal mark's editor. The title displays prominently on the map.

### Dispatch
Choose DISPATCH ALERT, tap a fictional location, edit the reason, and save. All players with that room open see the flashing notification and the dispatch marker. Tap ACKNOWLEDGE to hide your popup; the map mark remains until its creator removes it.

### Fullscreen
Press FULLSCREEN on the map header. Press EXIT FULLSCREEN to return. Use PAN MAP, +/- zoom and pinch zoom as before.

### Layouts
Press LAYOUTS near the map or INTERFACE STYLES in the sidebar. Select one of five profiles. The choice is remembered in this browser. THEMES / EDIT remains available for further changes and saving custom themes.
