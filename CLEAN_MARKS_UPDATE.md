# Border Patrol 1.7 — Cleaner Map Markers

The large words and incident-reason text have been removed from the map itself.

- Large incidents, dispatch alerts, and normal marks now use compact symbols.
- Blocked-area outlines remain visible without text painted over the map.
- Tap a mark or outlined area to see its title, full reason, case number, and mission confirmation controls.
- Incident search and the map-item list still show the full text.
- Dispatch flashes, small reports revealed on zoom, live marks, chat, radio, media, and the five layouts remain available.

This update changes the marker appearance only, not stored case data or team permissions.
