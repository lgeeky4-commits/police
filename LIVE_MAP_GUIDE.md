# Border Control — Live Mark and Incoming Route

- Join the four-player fictional field room.
- Press **LIVE MARK / OFF** to switch on instant marking. Each new map tap replaces your old live dot. A short arrow joins the previous position to the new one. At most **two direction arrows** remain for each player.
- Press **INCOMING ROUTE**, tap a fictional starting point, then tap its fictional destination. This creates a separate red arrow. Repeating the pair replaces your previous incoming arrow.
- Press **CLEAR MY LIVE MARKS** to remove both of your live layers.
- Everyone in the Border Control room sees these changes live; these are fictional reference points, not GPS positions or real border intelligence.
- The live layers disappear when the creator leaves. Regular checkpoint marks and media reports keep their previous behavior.
